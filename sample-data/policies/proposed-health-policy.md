### About this summary

This summary sets out the key features of the Essentials Select scheme proposed for Demo Organisation Ltd. It does not contain the full terms of the scheme, which are set out in the scheme rules. In the event of any difference between this summary and the scheme rules, the scheme rules will apply.

Throughout this summary, "we", "us" and "our" mean NorthStar Health plc, and "you" and "your" mean the person covered under the scheme.

## Section 1 - Key terms

The following terms have the meanings given below wherever they appear in this summary.

**Covered person** - an employee or a dependant who has been accepted for cover under the scheme.

**Employee** - a person employed by the company on a permanent contract, or on a fixed-term contract with an original duration of at least six months.

**Dependant** - an employee's spouse or partner, and their children up to the age of 21.

**Scheme year** - each period of twelve months beginning on the scheme start date or on a renewal date.

**Eligible treatment** - treatment of an acute condition that is medically necessary, carried out by a recognised practitioner and, where required, authorised by us in advance.

**Recognised practitioner** - a specialist, GP, therapist or counsellor who meets our professional and registration standards.

**Self-referral** - accessing a therapy without a referral from a GP or specialist.

Benefit limits apply to each covered person and are reset at the start of each scheme year.

## Section 3 - Who can be covered

The scheme is open to all employees of Demo Organisation Ltd who are resident in the United Kingdom or the Republic of Ireland. There is no minimum period of service before an employee can join, and cover is not restricted by grade or job role.

Contractors, consultants engaged through a service company, and agency workers are not employees for the purposes of the scheme and cannot be covered.

New employees may join from their first day of employment. Employees who do not join when first eligible may join at the next renewal date.

## Section 4 - Dependants

Employees may apply to add their spouse or partner and their children to the scheme.

Dependants may be added at the discretion of the scheme administrator. Arrangements for the cost of dependant cover, including whether any contribution will be required from the employee, will be confirmed at renewal.

Children can remain on the scheme until the renewal date following their 21st birthday.

## Section 5 - Your premium and excess

The annual premium for the scheme is £108,000, payable annually in advance on the scheme start date and on each renewal date. The premium includes insurance premium tax at the prevailing rate.

An excess of £150 applies to each covered person once in each scheme year. The excess applies to all claims under the scheme, including claims for cancer care, and is deducted from the first claim made in the scheme year.

## Section 6 - Hospital, out-patient and diagnostic cover

### Hospital treatment

Eligible in-patient and day-patient treatment is covered in full at hospitals in the NorthStar Select network. This includes accommodation, nursing, theatre costs, drugs and dressings, and the fees of the specialist and anaesthetist.

If you choose to be treated at a hospital outside the NorthStar Select network, you must obtain our approval before treatment, and we may limit the amount we pay to the cost of treatment at an equivalent network hospital.

### Out-patient consultations and diagnostics

Out-patient consultations with a specialist, together with diagnostic tests and scans, are covered in full following a referral from a GP or from the NorthStar digital GP service.

## Section 7 - Cancer care

We cover eligible cancer treatment in full, including surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and biological therapies, together with the diagnostic tests and consultations needed to diagnose and plan the treatment.

Drugs that are experimental, or that are not licensed for use in the United Kingdom for the condition being treated, are not covered.

Monitoring consultations and tests are covered for up to five years after active treatment has ended. Palliative care is covered where it is provided in a hospital or hospice in the NorthStar Select network.

## Section 8 - Therapies

Physiotherapy is covered for up to ten sessions per covered person in each scheme year when you have been referred by a GP or specialist.

You may also access physiotherapy by self-referral through the NorthStar member app. Where you self-refer, cover is limited to six sessions in each scheme year, and any further sessions will only be covered following a referral from a GP or specialist. Sessions accessed by self-referral count towards the overall limit of ten sessions.

Osteopathy and chiropractic treatment are not covered under this scheme.

## Section 9 - Mental health

Mental health treatment is covered in full. This includes out-patient therapy and counselling, psychiatric consultations, and in-patient and day-patient treatment.

Out-patient therapy is subject to a clinical review after every twelve sessions. Further sessions will be authorised where the review confirms that treatment remains clinically appropriate.

## Section 10 - Underwriting

The scheme is underwritten on a continued personal medical exclusions (CPME) basis. Employees transferring from the company's existing scheme will keep any personal exclusions that applied under that scheme.

New members joining after the scheme start date are covered on a moratorium basis. Conditions for which a new member has had symptoms, treatment or advice in the five years before joining are excluded until the member has completed one continuous year of cover without symptoms, treatment or advice for that condition.

## Section 11 - Exclusions

The scheme does not cover:

- treatment of chronic conditions, beyond the initial diagnosis and stabilisation of an acute flare-up;
- cosmetic treatment, unless it is needed as a direct result of an accident covered by the scheme;
- normal pregnancy and childbirth;
- routine dental treatment and optical care;
- experimental or unlicensed drugs and treatments;
- treatment received outside the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland;
- treatment for addiction or for self-inflicted injury;
- osteopathy and chiropractic treatment.

## Section 12 - How to claim

Claims can be made through the NorthStar member app or by calling 0800 555 0199. Treatment should be authorised before it takes place, and we will tell you which hospitals and practitioners you can use.

Invoices must be submitted within three months of the date of treatment. Invoices received after this time may not be paid.

## Section 13 - Complaints

If you wish to make a complaint, please write to NorthStar Health Complaints, 22 Meridian Way, Leeds LS1 4ZZ, or email complaints@northstar-health.example. If you are not satisfied with our final response, you may be able to refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

## Section 14 - General

The company must notify us of employees joining and leaving the scheme within 30 days. Cover for a covered person ends on the date the employee leaves the company's employment.
