## 2. Definitions

Certain words and phrases in this guide have a specific meaning. These are listed below and apply wherever the words appear.

**Acute condition** - a disease, illness or injury that is likely to respond quickly to treatment which aims to return you to the state of health you were in immediately before suffering the disease, illness or injury, or which leads to your full recovery.

**Chronic condition** - a disease, illness or injury that has one or more of the following characteristics: it needs ongoing or long-term monitoring; it needs ongoing or long-term control or relief of symptoms; it continues indefinitely; it has no known cure; or it is likely to come back.

**Dependant** - the member's spouse or partner, and any of their children under the age of 21, or under the age of 25 if in full-time education, who are named on the membership certificate.

**Employee** - a person employed by the policyholder under a contract of employment.

**Excess** - the amount you must pay towards the cost of eligible treatment before we pay a claim.

**Member** - an employee, or a dependant of an employee, who is named on the membership certificate.

**Policy year** - the period of twelve months starting on the effective date, or on any renewal date.

**Pre-existing condition** - any disease, illness or injury for which you have received medication, advice or treatment, or had symptoms of, in the five years before your cover started.

**Specialist** - a registered medical practitioner who holds a substantive consultant appointment in that speciality at an NHS hospital, or is recognised by us as a specialist.

**Therapist** - a physiotherapist, osteopath or chiropractor who is registered with the relevant statutory body and recognised by us.

## 3. Who can join the scheme

### 3.1 Employees

Membership is open to employees of the policyholder who meet all of the following conditions:

- they are employed on a permanent contract of employment;
- they normally live and work in the United Kingdom;
- they have completed at least three months of continuous service with the policyholder; and
- they hold a role at Grade 4 or above in the policyholder's grading structure.

Contractors, agency workers, interns and employees on fixed-term contracts are not eligible to join the scheme.

### 3.2 Dependants

An eligible employee may include their dependants on the scheme at the same time as they join, or at the next renewal date. A new spouse, partner or child may also be added within 30 days of the marriage, start of the partnership, birth or adoption.

The cost of cover for dependants is included in the scheme premium and is paid by the policyholder. No contribution is required from the employee for dependant cover.

### 3.3 When cover ends

Cover for a member ends on the earliest of: the date the employee leaves the employment of the policyholder; the date the employee stops meeting the eligibility conditions above; the date the policy is cancelled; or, for a dependant, the date they stop meeting the definition of a dependant.

## 4. Underwriting

This scheme is underwritten on a moratorium basis. This means that you do not need to complete a medical questionnaire when you join, but pre-existing conditions are not covered until you have completed a continuous period of two years of membership during which you have not had any symptoms of, medication for, or treatment or advice about that condition.

Once that two-year period has passed, the condition may become eligible for cover, subject to the other terms of the policy.

## 5. Premium and excess

### 5.1 Premium

The annual premium for the scheme at the effective date is £120,000. The premium is paid by the policyholder in twelve equal monthly instalments by direct debit. Insurance premium tax is included at the current rate.

The premium is reviewed at each renewal date and may change to reflect the claims experience of the scheme, the number of members and medical inflation.

### 5.2 Excess

An excess of £100 applies to each member once in each policy year. The excess is deducted from the first eligible claim the member makes in the policy year. If the first claim is for less than £100, the balance of the excess is deducted from later claims in the same policy year until the full excess has been paid.

The excess does not apply to claims for cancer treatment, to the NHS cash benefit or to the virtual GP service.

## 6. In-patient and day-patient treatment

We pay in full for eligible treatment received as an in-patient or day-patient at a hospital on the Atlas Standard hospital list. This includes:

- hospital accommodation in a private room with en-suite facilities;
- nursing care, drugs and dressings used while you are in hospital;
- theatre fees, and the fees of the specialist and anaesthetist; and
- diagnostic tests carried out while you are an in-patient or day-patient.

Where a child member under the age of 16 is admitted as an in-patient, we will also pay for one parent to stay in hospital with them.

Treatment must be for an acute condition and must be recommended by a specialist. Treatment received at a hospital that is not on the Atlas Standard list is not covered unless we have agreed to it in writing before the treatment takes place.

## 7. Out-patient treatment and diagnostics

Out-patient consultations with a specialist, and diagnostic tests carried out as an out-patient, are covered up to a combined limit of £1,000 per member per policy year. A referral from a GP is required before the first consultation.

MRI, CT and PET scans are paid in full when carried out at a recognised diagnostic centre following a referral by a specialist. Scans do not count towards the out-patient limit.

## 8. Therapies

Physiotherapy, osteopathy and chiropractic treatment are covered when you have been referred by a GP or a specialist and the treatment is carried out by a therapist.

Cover is limited to a combined total of eight sessions per member per policy year across all three therapies. Sessions beyond this limit are not covered unless they form part of a programme of rehabilitation following eligible in-patient surgery and have been authorised by us in advance.

Treatment for chronic conditions, maintenance therapy and treatment that is not expected to lead to an improvement in your condition are not covered.

## 9. Mental health

### 9.1 Out-patient mental health treatment

Out-patient treatment for mental health conditions, including psychiatric consultations and therapy such as cognitive behavioural therapy, is covered up to £1,500 per member per policy year. A referral from a GP is required.

### 9.2 In-patient and day-patient mental health treatment

In-patient and day-patient treatment for mental health conditions is covered for up to 28 days in each policy year, when recommended by a consultant psychiatrist and carried out at a hospital on the Atlas Standard list.

## 10. Cancer cover

Cancer treatment is paid in full. This includes:

- consultations, diagnostic tests and scans to diagnose cancer;
- surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and biological therapies;
- drugs licensed for use in the United Kingdom and prescribed by your specialist as part of your cancer treatment;
- palliative care to relieve symptoms where treatment is no longer expected to cure the cancer; and
- monitoring consultations and tests after active treatment has ended, for as long as your specialist recommends.

The excess does not apply to cancer treatment.

## 11. Additional benefits

### 11.1 NHS cash benefit

If you receive in-patient treatment as an NHS patient that would otherwise have been covered by this policy, we will pay you £100 for each night you spend in hospital, up to a maximum of 20 nights per policy year.

### 11.2 Virtual GP

All members have unlimited access to the Atlas virtual GP service by phone or video, 24 hours a day. Virtual GP appointments do not count towards the out-patient limit.

## 12. What is not covered

In addition to the limits and exclusions described elsewhere in this guide, the following are not covered:

- treatment of chronic conditions, beyond the initial diagnosis and treatment needed to stabilise an acute flare-up;
- pre-existing conditions, except as described in section 4;
- cosmetic or aesthetic treatment, unless it is needed to restore your appearance after an accident or after eligible cancer treatment;
- normal pregnancy and childbirth, although complications arising during pregnancy are covered;
- dental treatment and optical care, including eye tests, glasses and contact lenses;
- treatment received outside the United Kingdom;
- treatment for drug or alcohol misuse, or for self-inflicted injuries;
- experimental or unproven treatment, or drugs not licensed for use in the United Kingdom.

## 13. Making a claim

Before you arrange any treatment, contact Atlas Healthcare on 0800 555 0142 to tell us about your condition and obtain a pre-authorisation number. We will confirm whether the treatment is covered and which hospitals and specialists you can use.

Claims for treatment that has not been pre-authorised may not be paid. Invoices must be sent to us within six months of the date of treatment. We will pay the hospital and specialist directly wherever possible.

## 14. Complaints

If you are unhappy with any aspect of our service, please contact our customer relations team at Atlas Healthcare, Customer Relations, 1 Harbour Street, Bristol BS1 0AA. If we are unable to resolve your complaint, you may be able to refer it to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

## 15. General conditions

The policyholder must tell us about new employees joining the scheme and members leaving the scheme within 30 days. We may change the terms of the policy at any renewal date by giving the policyholder at least 30 days' notice.
